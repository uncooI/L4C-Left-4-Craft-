Left4Craft visual fix v2 - repairs invisible blocks and arrows

1. Close L4D1 and Prism.
2. Extract all contents beside your existing Launch.cmd and settings.json:
   D:\gpt stuf\Left4Craft\l4d1
   Allow replacement of the included files.
3. Double-click Apply-Visual-Fix.cmd.
4. Use your existing Launch.cmd. Allow Minecraft to start, then press F8.

Replaces the SceneEnd world renderer with the previously working EndScene
drawing path. Blocks and arrows draw before the Minecraft HUD. A separate
depth surface is filled from nearby solid BSP brushes to hide world objects
behind native floors and walls, without depending on L4D's postprocessed
depth buffer or a window-sized viewport at SceneEnd. Invisible player clips
are excluded from this visual depth pass. If a private depth surface cannot
be created, objects still draw, with native occlusion temporarily disabled.

Keeps Source point-light sampling, corrected field of view, QPC movement
sampling, native weapon suppression, and Minecraft arrow meshes.

Your Minecraft account, instance settings, mods and saved world are unchanged.
The previous DLL is backed up as bin/left4craft.pre-visual-v2.dll.

This DLL compiles and passes protocol and motion/FOV checks. In-game rendering
cannot be tested here. Native occlusion currently covers exported nearby solid
BSP geometry; moving props, displacements, survivors and infected are not part
of this private depth mesh. Flashlight shadows are not fully integrated.
